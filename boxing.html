The language

Punches by the numbers

1JabLead-hand straight. Your range-finder.
2CrossRear-hand straight. Turn the hip.
3Lead hookLead hand, elbow level, pivot the foot.
4Rear hookRear hand hook, rotate through.
5Lead uppercutLead hand, drive up from the legs.
6Rear uppercutRear hand, up the middle.

1-2-3 = jab, cross, lead hook. That's the whole shorthand — the voice calls them the same way.

Before you throw

Fundamentals

Stance

  • Lead foot forward, feet shoulder-width, slight stagger.
  • Weight on the balls of your feet, knees soft.
  • Hands up, elbows in, chin tucked behind the lead shoulder.

Guard

  • Gloves at cheekbone height, palms facing you.
  • Rear hand glued to the jaw, lead hand a touch forward.
  • Punch and it comes right back — never drop to reset.

Footwork

  • Step with the near foot first, drag the other to follow.
  • Never cross your feet or bounce flat-footed.
  • Small steps. Stay in stance the whole time.

Defense

  • Slip — small head move off-center, don't duck low.
  • Roll — bend at the knees, weave under.
  • Block / parry — catch or tap the shot, stay tight.

Make it count

Shadow boxing, done right

Warm up first

2–3 minutes: loosen shoulders, hips and ankles, then light footwork before the bell.

Snap, don't muscle

Throw fast and relaxed. Full extension, snap it back to guard. Breathe out on every punch.

Move between combos

Don't stand still. Step, pivot, reset your angle, then fire the next one the voice calls.

Heads up: general fitness content, not medical advice. Warm up, keep it pain-free, and check with a physician before starting a new exercise program — stop if you feel dizzy, short of breath, or any sharp pain.
