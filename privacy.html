Privacy Policy

Effective July 2, 2026
How Elite Metabolix handles your information. Elite Metabolix is general wellness, fitness, and nutrition coaching — not medical care and not pharmacy care. We collect only what we need to run your plan and coaching, we never sell your data, and you are always in control. This Policy works alongside our Terms of Use & Wellness Disclaimer.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us.

  • Account details, such as your name and email address.
  • Check-ins and inputs you log, such as sleep, weight, training sessions, perceived effort, energy, notes, and similar wellness information.
  • Photos you choose to submit — for example, a photo of your fridge or pantry for the Fridge Coach feature.
  • Messages you send us.

Wearable and health-app data (only if you connect it). If you choose to connect a wearable or health app, we access a limited set of your health and fitness data through the Google Health APIs (formerly the Fitbit APIs) — typically your activity (such as steps), sleep, and heart-rate information. We request read-only access, and only after you sign in to your own Google account and grant permission. You can disconnect at any time, which stops any further syncing.

Information collected automatically. As with most online services, we and our infrastructure providers may collect basic technical information (such as device and browser type and log data) needed to operate and secure the service.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • provide, personalize, and improve your coaching and the features you use, including your progress dashboard and any coaching messages;
  • prepare coaching content, which is reviewed by a human coach before it is sent to you;
  • communicate with you about the service and respond to your requests;
  • maintain the security, integrity, and proper functioning of the service; and
  • comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Google user data — Limited Use

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • We use Google Health data only to provide and improve the coaching features you request within Elite Metabolix.
  • We do not sell this data, and we do not use it for advertising or any advertising-related purpose.
  • We do not transfer this data to others except as necessary to provide or improve these features, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition — and always subject to appropriate confidentiality protections.
  • We do not allow humans to read this data except: (a) with your consent — for example, when your coach reviews the coaching prepared for you; (b) as necessary for security purposes; (c) to comply with applicable law; or (d) where the data has been aggregated and anonymized.

4. Who we share information with

We share information only with providers that help us operate Elite Metabolix, under agreements that limit their use of the data to providing services to us. These include:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage;
  • Anthropic — AI processing used to help prepare coaching drafts and to analyze photos you submit;
  • Google — the wearable and health-app data you choose to connect, via the Google Health APIs;
  • Resend — sending email;
  • Vercel — website and app hosting.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or in connection with a business transfer.

5. How we protect your information

Your data is stored with encryption in transit and at rest through our infrastructure providers, and access is restricted. Wearable connection tokens are stored securely and are never displayed to you or exposed publicly, and photos you submit are kept in private storage. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect a wearable at any time to stop new health-app data from syncing.
  • Access or delete your data. You can ask us for a copy of your personal information or ask us to delete it. On deletion, we remove your account data, check-ins, submitted photos, and wearable connection from our systems.
  • Correct information that is inaccurate.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights. California residents have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the CPRA, including the right to know, access, correct, and delete personal information, and to limit the use of sensitive personal information. Health-related information is treated as sensitive personal information. We do not “sell” or “share” personal information as those terms are defined under California law. To exercise any right, email hello@elitemetabolixhq.com.

7. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service, and then delete or anonymize it — except where we must retain it to comply with legal obligations.

8. Children

Elite Metabolix is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
